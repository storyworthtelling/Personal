Watch glyph studies

Each watch app is a glyph with no word beside it, in one fixed colour of its own. Point is the face and the lockdown, Dash stands in for Post, Wave for Score and Beat for Constitution. Two families follow: glyphs drawn from each app's name, and glyphs drawn from the face's own polygons. Each is shown large, at app-list size and smaller, and as the watch's app list.

The colours are a first proposal: a glass blue for Point, amber for Dash, violet for Wave and rose for Beat. They sit well apart on the colour wheel and differ in lightness, and no two are a red-green pair, so colour-blind eyes can still separate them. The shapes differ in every case anyway, so colour is never the only cue. The face's minute colour still sweeps the whole wheel and will pass through each of them once an hour. That is expected, since a glyph is never drawn on the face.
