Watch face studies, round 2

Round 2 develops studies 1, 5, 6 and 7, and tries study 1 as a 24-hour clock in two ways. Each study is shown at 2:22 am, 7:15 am, 1:41 pm and 7:50 pm, then with its date and with a text and a call arriving. People and messages are invented.

About notifications. You decided the face shows nothing anxious: no counts and no unread marks. So the face never changes when something arrives. On Wear OS the arriving text and the ringing call are cards that the system draws over the face, and a face cannot restyle them. The cards here are drawn in each study's own colours to show what P14's apps could look like, if the finding shows Post can draw them. If it cannot, they look like the stock Pixel cards.
