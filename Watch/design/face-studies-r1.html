Watch face studies, round 1

Ten ways to tell the time on the wrist without looking at anything else. Each face shows time and date only, nothing anxious, and has a very dim always-on view. These are studies for choosing a direction, not finished designs. They share nothing with Paper & Ink.

--
Real size: drag the calibration slider until this bar matches the short side of a bank card (54 mm). The watch outlines below then show true size.

Real sizes are approximate. Pixel Watch 2 (41 mm case) is drawn with a 30 mm display. The newer Pixel Watch is drawn at 41 mm and 45 mm with roughly 32 mm and 36 mm displays and thinner bezels. All of these get checked on the real watches. The sunrise and sunset in study 2 are invented fixed times. A real face would read the day's times on the watch, and nothing leaves it.
