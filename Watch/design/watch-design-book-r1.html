Watch design book

The visual companion to Watch/WATCH-DESIGN.md, the draft watch design language for Point, Dash, Wave and Beat. It shares nothing with Paper & Ink. Each section shows one open question (O1 to O6) with the proposed answer drawn, ready for your yes or your change. People and messages are invented.

Colour · O1, O2

Each app's day colour

Each app drifts across its own range once a day and back: at the night end at midnight, at the day end at noon, easing in between and never jumping. Each glyph below is drawn every three hours. Switch between the named colours and the on-black colours to see O2: maroon, navy and velvet nearly vanish on a black screen, so the proposal holds their lightness up and keeps their hue.

The mixing runs through a perceptual colour space along the shorter way round the colour wheel. Dash passes through plum on its way between navy and maroon. Wave's slate is so grey that its middle hours pass through a muted teal and olive on the way to amber. If you would rather it went the long way, through dusty rose, say so.

Point

The face, as chosen

Hours in sides, minutes in colour, with the afternoon as a second ring. The date sits under the shape in the shape's own colour. Always-on shows the shape in outline, without the date.

Touch · O4

Four buzzes, one meaning each

Each bar is one pattern over two seconds: the height is the strength and the width is the length. They differ in rhythm as well as in strength, so they can be told apart without looking. These shapes are a first guess, to be settled on the wrist.

Type · O3

One typeface, three sizes

The proposal is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, drawn for low-vision legibility, which suits a 30 mm screen. It is shown here at the three sizes the watch would use, on black.

Undo · O5

Filing a message, and taking it back

A filed message leaves at once. For six seconds an Undo line stays, drawn as Dash's glyph inside a thin ring that empties. Tap it to bring the message back, or let it run out and the filing stands. There are no confirmation dialogs.
