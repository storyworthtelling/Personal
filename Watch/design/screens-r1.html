Watch screens, round 2

Round 2 redraws only the tiles and Wave's Now playing, glyph-led as you chose; everything else is approved as it was. Every screen of Point, Dash, Wave and Beat on the round display, drawn in the approved watch language: true black, IBM Plex Mono, one short sentence at most, and each app in its day colour (drawn here at 4:20 pm). Each caption names the answer it follows (A#). Screens the watch itself draws (the call screen, the install prompt) are marked system. People, messages, music and workouts are invented.

The time on every app screen is drawn at the top, where Wear OS puts it. Colours follow the design book's day ranges at 4:20 pm, so at night Dash turns navy and Beat forest green. Album art is an invented gradient.
