Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026. This policy covers the log loop reader, a private tool described on the home page.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the storyworthtelling apps, on their own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else is asked to sign in to it.

What it reads

With the drive.readonly permission, the reader lists the folder named storyworthtelling-logs in the owner's Google Drive and downloads the diagnostic log files the owner's own apps put there. It does not open any other file. Those files are encrypted before they leave the phone, and the reader decrypts them with a key only the owner holds.

What it keeps

The decrypted logs exist only in a temporary workspace while the weekly run is in progress, and are deleted with it. Nothing from them is written to the code repository. What the run publishes there, in bug reports and fixes, is limited to sanitized descriptions of a problem's shape: message templates, error types and control names, with numbers, addresses, names and links removed.

What it shares

Data read by the reader is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. It is used only to find and fix bugs in the owner's own apps. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping it

The owner can withdraw the reader's access at any time from the third-party access page of their Google account. The apps keep working without it.
