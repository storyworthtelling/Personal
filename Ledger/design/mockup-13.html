Mockup 13 — the zero-based pages

Gate 3 · revision 1 · approved

Release E (D100, D152, D153): the worksheet gains a set-aside section and a zero-balance check, and when what's written doesn't balance, a follow-up page arrives the next day with suggestions. A4 at 1:1 in points, document palette, whole dollars. Figures are illustrative and add up.

The worksheet (a)

  • Set aside is new: the extra debt payment, named for the Snowball's current focus (D98), and one line per savings goal (D99). Each is a box like a category, with last month's figure printed and kept if left empty.
  • The target is printed once, in the intro: income less the Snowball payments ($3,100) is what the boxes should come to. Everything else on the top block is mockup 12's, unchanged.
  • To fit one sheet, rows go from 36 to 30pt with 26pt boxes (still over 9 mm to write in), and the write-in category lines drop from three to two (D157). With more categories than this, the page runs onto a second sheet rather than shrinking further.

The follow-up (b, c)

  • Only when it doesn't balance, and it arrives the next morning (D153). A page that balances gets no reply; the Budget tab just updates, silently (D89).
  • Over: the lines furthest above September are brought back first, largest rise first, never below September's figure, until the excess is gone; the last one takes only what's left (Gifts to $111, not $55). Set-aside lines are never trimmed (D156).
  • Under: the unassigned remainder is suggested for the extra debt payment, since that's what shortens the Snowball; savings lines are shown with their boxes so it can be split instead (D156).
  • Empty box takes the suggestion, so agreeing costs no writing, the same rule as the worksheet. A figure the suggestion changes is struck through, not erased, so you can see what changed; a dash means the line stands as written.
  • Everything else is printed for reference, not in boxes, and any of it can be changed on the two “change another line” rows: a name and a figure, read the same way as a write-in category.
  • Until it balances, the Budget tab tracks what was written (D156): the limits are live from the first read, and only the zero check is pending. The follow-up repeats each morning until the figures balance, and each new page replaces the last on the reMarkable (D60).
