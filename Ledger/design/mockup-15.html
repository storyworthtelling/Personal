Mockup 15 — editing an account

Gate 3 · revision 1 · approved

Edits happen on the account's own card (D111), on the post-it that tapping it already opens (mockup 3), so there is no manage-accounts screen and no edit mode. A connected account can only be marked as savings (D56); a hand-entered one can have everything filled in later. Drawn 1:1 in dp.

What can be edited

  • A connected account: one switch. Its figures are Plaid's, so the post-it offers only what Plaid can't know: whether it counts as Savings (D56). The switch starts from Plaid's own subtype and says so (“Plaid calls it savings”); turning it over is the owner's override and moves the account on or off the Savings tab. Named for the thing, not the verb (§11); on in the identity green (§7).
  • A hand-entered account: its fields, on the post-it. The same design-system boxes as mockup 4, set on the yellow post-it instead of an index card. Balance, APR, Payment, Every and Next due; APR and Payment carry the required tint until both are there, because until then it's not in the Snowball, and the post-it says so in its first line.
  • Nothing to save. Each field keeps what's typed when you leave it, and the card behind updates at once: “No payment yet” becomes “Due Oct 15 · $420”. That's the success state (§7), with no Save button and no toast. A bad figure turns the box rose with a line saying how to fix it, as in mockup 4.
  • Balance shows when it was last set. A loan Post can read a statement for (D15) updates itself and says “From Post, Sep 14” instead.
  • Remove is on hand-entered accounts only, at the foot in the problem rose. It's undo-first: the card goes at once and the dog-ear holds the undo (§13). A connected account leaves with its bank, from the bank's heading (mockup 2).
  • The keyboard pushes the post-it up so the field being typed in stays in view; the pinned bar hides while it's open, as it does on the form in mockup 4.
