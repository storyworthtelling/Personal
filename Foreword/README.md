# Foreword

An Android (MAUI) app — formerly DailyLoader, renamed in P9 — that delivers three documents to a reMarkable
tablet before the owner wakes (The Day, the calendar page; The Brief, the news, opinion, newsletters, comics and
the NYT crossword; and Prayer, the journal page) and reads what is written on them back into Google Calendar,
Tasks and the encrypted prayer archive. Credentials are entered in-app on the Settings page.

- Tests: `dotnet test Foreword/Foreword.Tests/Foreword.Tests.csproj -p:ForewordTfms=net10.0`
- APK builds in CI via `.github/workflows/build-android.yml`
- UI follows the repo-wide Paper & Ink design bible (`DESIGN.md` at the repo
  root); the tokens and components come from the shared `Storyworthtelling.Maui` kit
  (no palette of its own), and the launcher icon is generated by `Tools/IconStudio`.
